Video & Photo Editor
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Bashir Sons of Apps

Who we are

Video & Photo Editor (package com.farhan.videoeditor) is made by Farhan Bashir — Bashir Sons of Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

Editing and exporting happen on your phone. The app needs no account, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and your photos, videos and audio are not uploaded to us.

Data on your device

Projects, thumbnails, recorded voice-overs, generated voices, stickers and settings are stored inside the app's private storage on your phone. Exported videos and saved photos go to your gallery (Movies / Pictures › VideoEditor). You can delete projects in the app; uninstalling the app deletes its private data.

Permissions

  • Photos, videos and audio — only the files you pick, to edit them.
  • Camera — Beauty Camera and taking photos inside the app.
  • Microphone — recording a voice-over, only while you record.
  • Notifications and foreground service — showing export progress while a video is being saved.
  • Internet — only for the optional AI Story Video feature (see below).

You can turn any permission off in your phone's settings; the related feature will then not work.

AI Story Video (optional)

If you use AI Story Video, you enter your own Google Gemini API key. The key is stored only on your phone. Your story text / prompts are sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API to create the script and pictures, and the results come back to your phone. We do not receive this data. Google's handling is covered by Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms. If you don't use this feature, nothing is sent.

Google ML Kit and phone services

Beauty / face tools and background removal use Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone; Google may collect anonymous diagnostic and performance data about the ML Kit component. Auto captions use your phone's speech-recognition service and voice tools use your phone's text-to-speech engine; depending on your phone and language these system services (usually Google) may process audio or text online under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your files stay on your device and are protected by Android's app sandbox. Keep your phone locked and your API key private.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
