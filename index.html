Puffjar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Developer: Farhan Bashir (Bashir Sons of Apps)

1. About this policy

Puffjar ("the app") is a casual puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we do not collect

Puffjar does not require an account or sign-in. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

3. Information we collect for online features (Google Firebase)

To provide a leaderboard and to understand how the game is used, the app uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore). When you play, the app sends the following to Firebase:

  • An anonymous random user ID created by Firebase (not linked to your name, email or phone number).
  • The player name and icon you choose in the game, and your best score. These are shown publicly on the leaderboard to other players, so please do not use your real full name or any personal details as your player name.
  • Usage statistics: the date you first used the app, the total time spent in the app, the number of games played, and the time you were last active. These are visible only to the developer and are used to understand how many people use the game and to improve it.

The app and the SDKs it uses (Firebase and Google Mobile Ads) may also automatically collect crash logs and diagnostic information (for example app crashes, performance data and device/OS version) so that we can keep the game stable, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region level only, never precise location).

Firebase is operated by Google; see the Firebase privacy information. You can delete your online data at any time in the app under Settings → Delete my online data, or by emailing us (see Contact).

4. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked skins, settings such as sound, music and volume, your chosen profile name and icon, and your reminder time) is stored locally on your device. It stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This may be used to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure performance and to prevent fraud. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. The only data shared with a third party is the data that Google AdMob and its partners collect to serve and measure ads, as described above. We do not use or share your data for any other advertising purpose.

Ad types used: interstitial ads (for example after a game ends) and rewarded ads (which you choose to watch to earn a reward such as an extra power-up or a daily gift).

6. Consent and your ad choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later from Settings → Ad privacy options when that option is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one local notification per day on your device at the time you choose. These notifications are created by the app on your device and are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your Android system settings.

8. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to show ads and to use the leaderboard.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service as described above.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional daily reminder.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback in the game, which you can turn off in Settings.

9. Music credits

Background music: "Soft Piano" by Leberch and "Holiday Bells" by Alex Morgan, obtained from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License.

10. Children

Puffjar is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can help.

11. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Online data (leaderboard entry and usage statistics) is kept until you delete it with Settings → Delete my online data, or ask us to delete it by email; because the ID is anonymous, please tell us your player name and approximate date of first use so we can find it. For data held by Google for ads, use your Google account and Android privacy controls.

12. Security

Data sent to Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that each player can change only their own record and usage statistics cannot be read by other players. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features change. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above.

14. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or other regions with privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent for personalised ads and to complain to your local data protection authority. You can delete your online data from the app (Settings → Delete my online data) or contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.
